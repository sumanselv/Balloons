Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pop Balloons ("we", "us", "the site") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name or email address just to play. The balloons, sounds and animations all run in your own browser.

Contact form. If you use the Contact form, you give us your name, email address and message. The form is processed by a third-party service, Web3Forms, which forwards your message to our email inbox. We use this information only to read and reply to your message. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the message.

Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and the other services described below may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can also control or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block cookies, the game will still work, but ads may be less relevant.

Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before using cookies or similar technologies for personalized ads, using a consent message provided through Google. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy options shown on the site or in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google AdSense shows advertisements.
  • Google Fonts supplies the handwriting-style font used on the scraps of paper. Loading the font sends your IP address and browser details to Google.
  • Web3Forms delivers messages sent through the Contact form.
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control how they handle your data.

Browser storage

The game itself does not store your progress, and it does not save cookies or local storage of its own. The third-party services above may set their own cookies as described in this policy.

Children's privacy

Pop Balloons is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted. Because we only receive information when you send us a message, you can ask us to delete your message at any time using the Contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, go to the game and use the Contact button in the top-left corner or in the footer.
